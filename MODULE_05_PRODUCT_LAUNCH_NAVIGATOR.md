# Module 05 — Ayurveda Product Launch Navigator

## 1. Purpose
To help Ayurveda entrepreneurs, researchers and innovators explore product ideas, market opportunities, intellectual property and regulatory requirements.

## 2. Main Features

1. Generate Ayurveda-related product ideas.
2. Explore medicinal plants and potential ingredients.
3. Retrieve traditional Ayurvedic references.
4. Explore modern scientific research related to ingredients.
5. Research existing products and competitors.
6. Search relevant patent databases.
7. Explore intellectual property opportunities.
8. Identify country-specific regulatory requirements.
9. Create preliminary product development roadmaps.
10. Organize research and product ideas.

## 3. Product Research Information

Each product research record should contain:

- Product concept
- Intended product category
- Target audience
- Proposed ingredients
- Traditional references
- Scientific research references
- Existing market products
- Potential differentiation
- Patent and prior-art search results
- Regulatory considerations
- Source references
- Research status

## 4. Example User Journey

A user wants to explore an Ayurvedic herbal skincare product.

The platform should:

1. Collect the product idea.
2. Identify relevant herbs and ingredients.
3. Retrieve traditional references.
4. Retrieve relevant scientific research.
5. Explore existing market products.
6. Search relevant patents and prior art.
7. Identify applicable regulatory considerations.
8. Generate a preliminary development roadmap.

## 5. Interface Requirements

- Product idea input
- Ingredient explorer
- Research results
- Market research section
- Patent search section
- Regulatory information panel
- Product roadmap
- Saved projects

## 6. Important Rules

- Never guarantee patentability.
- Do not present AI-generated patent searches as exhaustive.
- Distinguish product research from legal advice.
- Distinguish traditional claims from scientific evidence.
- Show official regulatory sources.
- Clearly identify the country and date of regulatory information.
- Respect traditional knowledge and intellectual property rights.

## 7. Initial MVP

Start with:
- Product idea input
- Ingredient research
- Scientific research references
- Basic market research
- Preliminary patent and regulatory source links
- Simple product development roadmap
